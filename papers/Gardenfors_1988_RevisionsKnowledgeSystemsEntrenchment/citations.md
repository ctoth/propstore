## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Alchourrón, Gärdenfors, and Makinson (1985)**: the AGM contraction/revision representation result on which the paper builds.
- **Gärdenfors (1984)**: develops epistemic importance as the qualitative basis for minimal belief change.
- **Grove (1986)**: alternative semantic modelling of theory change, useful when comparing entrenchment with systems of spheres.
