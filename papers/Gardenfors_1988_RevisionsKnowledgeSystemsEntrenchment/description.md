Introduces epistemic entrenchment as a qualitative ordering over propositions relative to a deductively closed knowledge set and proves a representation correspondence between constrained entrenchment orderings and AGM contraction/revision operators. For Propstore, it rules out treating arbitrary atom/source/context/kind prefix weights as epistemic entrenchment: source reliability and contextual preference require separately modelled evidence or prioritized-information owners, while entrenchment must obey the logical constraints of the represented belief state.
