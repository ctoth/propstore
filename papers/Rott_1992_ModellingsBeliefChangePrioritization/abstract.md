## Our Interpretation

Rott studies syntax-based belief change where a prioritized base of explicit beliefs generates a logically closed belief set. Base priority and epistemic entrenchment are different semantic objects: priority orders explicit base items, while entrenchment expresses comparative retractability of beliefs and must respect logical relationships. Mappings between them are mediated by preferences among maximal sub-bases and contraction operators, and are approximate outside important special cases.
