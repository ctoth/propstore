## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Gärdenfors and Makinson (1988)**: the standard proposition-level epistemic-entrenchment representation.
- **Nebel (1992)**: prioritized syntax-based revision and the converse direction between priorities and entrenchment.
- **Dubois and Prade (1991)**: numeric/possibilistic representation of epistemic entrenchment, relevant only if Propstore later designs a formal strength scale.
- **Rott (1992b)**: generalized entrenchment without the standard totality/modularity requirement.
