Separates prioritized belief bases from epistemic entrenchment and develops formal mappings between them through preferences over maximal non-implying sub-bases and contraction behavior. The paper is decisive for Propstore's issue #63: source/context/kind weights could at most be inputs to an explicitly owned prioritized belief base, and cannot be treated directly as epistemic entrenchment or as a per-call override of revision semantics.
