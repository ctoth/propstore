## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Vilain 1982** - origin of the d/s/f subdivision giving 13 relations; adds consistency maintenance and time points (PDF pp.8, 18).
- **Kahn and Gorry 1977** - reference events in before/after chaining; the direct ancestor of reference intervals (PDF pp.5, 14).
- **Freuder 1982** - higher-order consistency needed to close the three-node consistency gap (PDF p.12).
- **Doyle 1979** - truth maintenance needed to manage persistent-interval assumptions (PDF p.22).
- **McDermott 1982** - the more expressive point-based temporal logic Allen contrasts with (PDF pp.5, 18).
