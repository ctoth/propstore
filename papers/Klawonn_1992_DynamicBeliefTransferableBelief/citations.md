## Key Citations for Follow-up

- Smets 1990 (IEEE PAMI) — the companion algebraic justification of the combination rule; already in collection.
- Smets 1991c — disjunctive rule and generalized Bayesian theorem, source of the disjunctive results used in section 8; collection has the 1993 journal version.
- Kruse and Schwecke 1990 — origin of the specialization concept for belief functions.
- Yager 1986 — entailment principle and the result that specializations are at least as committed.
- Gardenfors 1988 — expansion/contraction framework whose commutativity requirement this paper adopts.
