Journal version of the propositional logic of context: ist(κ, φ) over context sequences with per-context partial vocabularies, a Hilbert system (PL, K, Δ, MP, CS) proved sound and complete, and correspondence results for consistent (D), truth (Tr), flat (4⁻¹, 5⁻¹) and meaninglessness-as-falsity (MF) variants. Adds a finite-model-property proof that validity in a context is decidable and representation theorems matching actual context models to multi-modal Kripke models and Δ-satisfying (C₁) Kripke models back to context models. Gives propstore's ist(c, p) contexts a decidable propositional semantics in which out-of-vocabulary claims are neither true nor false unless the MF variant is chosen explicitly.
