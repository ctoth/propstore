## Key Citations for Follow-up

- Guha 1991: the thesis whose validity reading of ist and definedness (meaningless-as-false) this paper formalizes; already in the collection.
- McCarthy 1993: the original ist proposal (ist as truth, lifting).
- Shoham 1991: the rival approach (every proposition meaningful everywhere; ist via current-context implication).
- Halpern & Moses 1992: the modal knowledge/belief logics this system collapses to with one context.
- Mints 1992: the style of decision procedure proposed as future work.
