Presents an argument-based extended logic programming system with strict and defeasible rules, strong and weak negation, rebutting and undercutting defeat (undercut ignores priorities and beats rebuttal), and priorities compared over the defeasible rules relevant to each conflict, including conflicts mediated by strict rules. Gives a grounded fixpoint semantics with justified, overruled and defensible arguments and a sound and complete dialectical proof theory, then makes the priorities defeasible by lifting justified ≺ conclusions into the defeat relation step by step, with credulous stable and defensible-extension variants and a proof that it yields strictly more conclusions than Dung's well-founded semantics. Directly informs propstore's ASPIC+ layer and render-time preference handling, where rule priorities such as lex superior, posterior and specialis are themselves argued over rather than fixed.
