## Key Citations for Follow-up

- **Dubois and Prade (1991)**: formal link between possibilistic certainty levels and epistemic entrenchment.
- **Konieczny and Pino-Pérez (2002)**: representation theory for flat merging under integrity constraints, lifted here to prioritized merging.
- **Shafer (1976)**: source-reliability interpretation that the paper carefully distinguishes from likelihood.
- **Darwiche and Pearl (1997)**: iterated-revision postulates; especially the controversial C2 that forces total override.
